HALLOWEEN JELLY — ORIGINAL AUDIO

Pumpkin Tiptoe — 106 BPM, marimba, 36.23s.
Moonflower Bells — 90 BPM, celesta, 42.67s.
A Tiny Pumpkin Carousel — 114 BPM, calliope waltz, 25.26s.

All three scores and instrument synthesis were locally authored for this project using the existing original CatSound PCM instrument kit. MP3 audio is embedded in index.html. No third-party music, recordings, voices, samples or remote audio service. Feedback effects use locally synthesised filtered noise and tones.

Music and effects start only after a user gesture, have independent controls and volume settings, and stop with gameplay pause or page hiding.
